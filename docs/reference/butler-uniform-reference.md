# Butler Uniform Reference

Use `assets/reference/butler-uniform-approved.jpg` as the approved visual reference for any butler, concierge, or service staff appearing in project imagery.

## Approved Direction

- Deep navy tailored butler jacket and matching trousers.
- Champagne gold trims, piping, buttons, cuffs, and shoulder details.
- Structured standing collar.
- Matching navy cap with gold accent.
- White gloves when the staff member is actively serving, standing formally, or handling visitor items.
- Black polished formal shoes.
- Name badge or small service badge may appear in gold tone.
- Overall look: formal, calm, premium, disciplined, and aligned with Kingdom Centre hospitality.

## Accepted Variations

- Version 1: cleaner front jacket with pocket accents and simple gold trim.
- Version 2: more ceremonial double-breasted jacket with stronger gold cuff and shoulder details.

Both versions are acceptable. Avoid mixing in unrelated hotel suits, casual blazers, generic reception clothing, or black-only uniforms.

## Image Generation Rule

When creating or replacing any image that includes visible staff, the staff uniform must follow this reference before any other styling instruction.
